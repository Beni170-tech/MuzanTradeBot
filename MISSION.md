# MISSION.md

## Project: MuzanTradeBot
## Mode: Guardian Mode

### Mission Statement

Build a professional Telegram crypto trading assistant that protects capital first.

This bot is designed to operate in Guardian Mode, prioritizing capital preservation above all else. Every feature, every trade, and every decision must pass through multiple layers of safety checks before execution.

### Core Principles

1. **Capital Protection First** - Never risk more than we can afford to lose
2. **Safety Over Speed** - Take time to verify rather than rush to execute
3. **Systematic Approach** - Build and test one feature at a time
4. **Transparent Operations** - Log everything, explain every decision
5. **Continuous Improvement** - Learn from every trade, win or lose

### Guardian Mode Protocol

When operating in Guardian Mode:
- All trades require passing risk assessment checks
- Position sizing is calculated based on current portfolio health
- Stop-loss levels are mandatory for every position
- Emergency exits are always available and tested
- No trade executes without multiple confirmation signals
